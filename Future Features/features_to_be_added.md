# AislePilot Product Roadmap & Future Features

This document outlines upcoming feature expansions for **AislePilot**, focusing on family sharing, multi-user authentication, active trip persistence, and historical trip analytics.

---

## 👥 1. Family Multi-User Portal & Shared Accounts

* **Family Account Authentication**:
  - Secure login portal enabling all family members (e.g., Mom, Dad, Kids, Roommates) to access a shared family workspace.
  - Individual user profiles with custom preferences, favorite items, and personal activity tags.
* **Real-Time Shared List Syncing**:
  - Add items to a shared family grocery list from separate phones in real-time.
  - Live in-store sync: As one family member checks off an item in Aisle A4, it automatically updates on everyone else's screen.

---

## 💾 2. Active Trip Persistence & Favorite List Templates

* **Seamless Trip State Memory**:
  - Auto-save active shopping trips to the cloud / browser storage.
  - Close the browser or switch apps mid-trip and return anytime without losing checked-off items or your active store walking path.
* **Saved List Templates**:
  - Save frequently used grocery runs (e.g., *"Sunday Family Staples"*, *"BBQ Cookout"*, *"Baking Run"*) for 1-click quick loading.

---

## 📊 3. Trip History & Efficiency Analytics

* **Past Trips Archive**:
  - Searchable timeline of all completed grocery trips, including store location (Anna TX vs Melissa TX), item counts, and completion dates.
* **Store Efficiency Dashboard**:
  - Metrics tracking total walking distance saved (in meters/feet), estimated time saved, and frequent purchase trends.
